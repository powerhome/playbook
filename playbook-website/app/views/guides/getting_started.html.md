title: Getting Started
---

Playbook is an open source design system built for Ruby on Rails and React, with additional support for SwiftUI, HTML/CSS, and Figma. Playbook is built & maintained by the User Experience & Development teams at [Power Home Remodeling](https://powerhrg.com/). Playbook is packaged as a [RubyGem](https://rubygems.org/gems/playbook_ui) and [NPM package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/playbook-ui) and works with a variety of setups.

Start with the setup guide that matches your stack, then review [Dependencies](/guides/getting_started/dependencies) for optional packages (icons, charts, maps, and more).
